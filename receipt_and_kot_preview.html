Key System Architecture & Printing Features

The Ice Talk POS printing system uses an off-screen continuous thermal printer renderer configured for 80mm/78mm (285px) and 58mm (216px) ESC/POS paper rolls.

1. Customer Receipt (Bill) Printed at the cashier desk upon settlement. Features the Ice Talk emblem, complete Puttalam contact details, cashier name, itemized pricing, calculated subtotal, automatic discount breakdown, payment method (Cash/Card/Online), tendered cash, and exact change calculation.
2. Single Printer Mode (Master KOT) When only 1 thermal printer is connected at the restaurant, the system automatically groups line items under distinct inverted black section headers: [RICE & KITCHEN], [JUICE & DESSERTS], and [BUNS & SHORT EATS].
3. Multi-Station Split Routing When station printers are enabled in the POS settings, a mixed order (e.g. Rice + Juice + Bun) is automatically split and routed simultaneously to the respective printers in under 1 second without disturbing the other stations.
4. Special Kitchen Instructions Any customization added by the waiter (e.g. "Extra spicy" or "Less sugar") prints in italics with an asterisk directly beneath the respective item on both the KOT and the bill.
